## Round 2 — ShapleyMCG path (declared 06:27, before any round-2 allocation or causal arm was run)

7. 06:09 Brandon's correction: the pilot's allocation used the uncalibrated proxy only; the required path is the ShapleyMCG one — measured end-to-end KLD attributed to units (path-integrated Aumann-Shapley), THEN allocation, with the sparse downgrade tier at 4:8, the block-16 rotation calibrated on the calibration data, causal layer-by-layer re-encode, and end-to-end KLD scored the same way as the TR3/EXL3 control. Round 2 re-runs the same path with those changes; the pilot's B8* arms stand only as the pre-registered/post-hoc proxy results they are.
8. Sparse tier: T0 is now 4:8 pair-structured (2 of 4 adjacent pairs kept per 8 elements; 0.5 metadata bits/element; group-16 scales per logical row). VERIFIED 06:29 against PTX ISA 9.3 (pdf, sections 'Sparse mma.sp::ordered_metadata operating on .e2m1 type with .kind::mxf4 or .kind::mxf4nvf4' and 9.7.17.10.8.3 for tcgen05): 'matrix A is pair-wise structured sparse at a granularity of 4:8 ... each two-wide sub-chunk within the eight-wide chunk must be all zeroes or all non-zeros ... positions of the two two-wide sub-chunks with non-zero values ... indicated by two 2-bit indices in the metadata' — i.e. 2 of 4 adjacent pairs, 4 metadata bits per 8 elements (0.5 bit/element), exactly what T0's mask and byte accounting use. GPTQ decides the pair mask by SparseGPT pair saliency on the original (unrotated) layout, then applies error feedback in the rotated basis.
9. Rotation calibration (fit role only): per layer, Cayley-parameterized block-16 rotations (R_in shared by gate/up, R_mid for down) trained against the full-expert output error on the 256 stored routed samples per expert (route-weight^2 weighted) with the NVFP4 quantizer in the loop; the basis per layer is the best of {I, Had16, learned-from-I, learned-from-Had16} by that objective. Decision rule for the round-2 basis (selection role, W4A16, uniform T1, GPTQ): B5L (calibrated-learned) vs B5 (Had16) — the lower mean KLD wins; ties (CI covers zero) keep Had16 (pre-registered basis).
10. Attribution: provisional endpoint = B5-prov (uniform NVFP4, Had16, GPTQ) — the same weights as B5. Straight path W(a) = W_src + a (W_end - W_src) over ALL routed-expert weights; 5 Gauss-Legendre nodes; fit role, 32 windows (two halves on two GPUs, window-weighted merge); share a_u = sum_k w_k <dKLD/dW_u(a_k), delta_u>; the residual (KLD_end - KLD_src - sum a_u) is reported, not hidden.
11. Calibrated allocation (repository rule): per unit, scale = a_u / proxy_u(T1); every tier's proxy is multiplied by that scale; a non-negative per-unit offset keeps values finite; units with ~0 proxy fall back to the layer-median scale; negative shares are kept (they lower the unit's cost) and counted. Exact-byte MCKP at E1b and E1a bytes. Control B7u = same candidates, uncalibrated proxy, E1b bytes.

14. 06:40 Refinement to item 11, decided before any calibrated allocation ran, after seeing the tiny smoke test (1 of 48 shares negative): a negative Aumann-Shapley share means the unit's quantization error slightly LOWERED KLD along the path — noise around zero, not evidence that sparsifying it further would help.
